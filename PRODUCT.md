# ResumeIQ — PRODUCT.md (Impeccable design context)

## What it is
ResumeIQ is an AI resume-intelligence platform with three portals sharing one
scoring/matching engine: candidate, recruiter, admin.

## Users & goals
- **Candidate** (job seeker, student–senior): upload resume → understand ATS score
  in plain language → match against a JD → improve with guarded AI rewrites.
  Goal: clarity and trust, never fabricated achievements.
- **Recruiter** (hiring manager, time-poor): upload JD → bulk-upload resumes →
  ranked table, side-by-side compare, skill heatmap, AI shortlist with reasons.
  Goal: fast, explainable shortlisting.
- **Admin** (platform owner): usage / storage / token-cost dashboards, roles,
  system health. Goal: cost control on free-tier stack.

## Brand / product lane
Product UI (app dashboards + tools), not marketing site. Calm, precise,
document-like. Trust > flash. No purple gradients, no glow, no bounce easing.

## Voice
Plain, direct, honest about limits (cold starts, AI busy, flagged content).
Show "waking up the server…" states, never a bare spinner. Flagged AI content
is visibly distinct, never hidden.

## Anti-references
No generic AI-slop landing (centered hero + 3 cards + purple gradient).
No dark-mode-only glow aesthetics. No auto-applied rewrites.
